## 2. statement ☀️
**On screen:** Sunlight: All Colours

**Says:** Sunlight is actually all colours mixed together.

## 3. statement 🌌
**On screen:** Rayleigh Scattering

**Says:** When sunlight hits air molecules, it scatters everywhere. This is Rayleigh scattering.

## 4. bullets 💡
**On screen:** Why Blue Scatters More
- Shorter wavelength
- Scatters 10x more

**Says:** Blue light scatters 10 times more than red light because it's shorter wavelength.

## 5. stat 🔵
**On screen:** Why Sky Looks Blue · **All directions**

**Says:** Scattered blue light comes from all directions, so sky looks blue.

## 6. statement 🌅
**On screen:** Sunset Magic

**Says:** At sunset, light travels far through air, so blue scatters away and red/orange remains.
