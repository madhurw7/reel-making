## 3. answer ✨
**On screen:** Venus

**Says:** It is Venus. It takes 243 Earth days to spin once on its axis.

## 4. compare ⏳
**On screen:** Day vs Year
- Rotation: 243 Days
- Orbit: 225 Days

**Says:** Venus takes 243 days to rotate, but only 225 days to orbit the Sun.

## 5. statement 🌅

**Says:** Venus also spins backwards. This means the Sun rises in the west.
